The Art of Leathercraft

Born from Tuscan Soil, Crafted for a Lifetime

At Maison Vélure, every bag begins in century-old Tuscan tanneries along the Arno River. We reject chrome synthetics in favor of organic vegetable tanning using chestnut and mimosa barks—producing leather that grows richer, softer, and more characterful with every passing year.

  • Saddle Stitching: Double-needle linen thread stitches that never unravel, even under heavy tension.
  • Hand-Painted Edge Lacquer: Seven protective layers cured between hand-sanding rituals.
  • 24K Gold Plated Brass: Solid forged hardware immune to tarnishing or corrosion.
