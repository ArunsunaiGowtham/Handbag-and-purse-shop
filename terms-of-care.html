Terms of Care & Artisan Warranty

Preserving the structural integrity, hand-stitched seams, and lifetime patina of your handbag.

Generational Craftsmanship Covenant

Care & Lifetime Longevity

Certified Florence Atelier Standard • 100% Full-Grain Tuscan Leather Guidelines

1. Lifetime Artisan Guarantee

Every Maison Vélure handbag is backed by our lifetime warranty covering structural defects: hand-saddled seams, solid brass buckles, zipper slider mechanisms, and edge burnishing. Authentic repairs are performed exclusively in Florence.

2. Natural Full-Grain Hides

We select full-grain calfskin tanned with vegetable extracts. Subtle variations in pore density, faint wrinkles, and natural tone shifts are hallmarks of pure uncorrected leather and not defects; they will deepen into a rich, lustrous patina.

3. Archival Storage & Rest

When not in use, lightly stuff your bag with acid-free tissue to retain silhouette geometry and place it in the complimentary archival cotton dustbag. Shield from radiators, heaters, and direct sun to prevent moisture depletion.

4. Rain & Liquid Protocol

If exposed to unexpected rainfall, immediately blot (do not rub) using a clean, soft microfibre cloth. Allow the leather to dry naturally at room temperature. Never use synthetic detergents, baby wipes, or artificial hot air.

5. Complimentary Annual Atelier Leather Spa

All registered clients are entitled to one annual complimentary leather refreshment session. Our master restorers provide:

  • Natural beeswax conditioning & steam hydration
  • Edge-paint sealant inspection & touch-ups
  • Ultrasonic hardware polishing & clasp re-tensioning

Need Leather Spa Care or Restoration?

Our atelier leather technicians are ready to examine your handbag, provide conditioning advice, or schedule an annual spa appointment.
