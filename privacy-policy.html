Privacy & Client Data Charter

Our commitment to discretion, transparent data handling, and uncompromising confidentiality.

Atelier Confidentiality Standards

Respecting Your Personal Privacy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Certified GDPR & International Privacy Compliant

1. Information We Collect

We only collect essential details necessary to handcraft and deliver your pieces: your name, shipping address, email, phone number, and optional monogramming choices. We never collect sensitive personal information without your explicit instruction.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is exclusively utilized to process orders, generate lifetime certificates of authenticity, arrange white-glove courier deliveries, and provide VIP Concierge assistance. We never sell, rent, or trade your data with external advertisers.

3. Payment & Encryption

All transactions are processed through end-to-end 256-bit encrypted gateways conforming to PCI-DSS Level 1 standards. Maison Vélure Paris never stores, logs, or views your complete credit card or payment credentials on internal systems.

4. Cookies & Site Preferences

We strictly deploy minimal, functional cookies required to sustain your session: preserving items in your shopping bag, saving your curated wishlist, and remembering your chosen visual theme (Light/Dark mode and RTL layout preferences).

5. Your Rights & Data Ownership

Under European GDPR and international privacy regulations, you retain total ownership of your information. You may at any moment request:

  • A complete export of your client profile
  • Immediate update of your delivery address
  • Permanent erasure of your account records

Have Inquiries Regarding Your Privacy?

Our dedicated Atelier Privacy Officer is available to assist you with data requests, account erasure, or confidentiality questions.
