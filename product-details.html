Vegetable-Tanned Calfskin Preservation

Your bag will develop a richer caramel patina over time. To maintain water repellency and suppleness, follow these atelier recommendations:

  • Condition quarterly with our beeswax leather balm using gentle circular strokes.
  • If caught in rain, pat dry immediately with a clean lint-free cloth; avoid hair dryers or radiators.
  • Store upright in the provided organic cotton dustbag stuffed with acid-free tissue.
