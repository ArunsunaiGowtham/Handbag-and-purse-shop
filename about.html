The Art of Timeless Leathercraft

Founded in Florence in 1984, Maison Vélure was born from a singular passion: creating handbags of uncompromised structural integrity and everlasting beauty.

Our Origins

From an Arno River Workshop to the Global Stage

Our founder, Henri Vélure, apprenticed under master saddle-makers in the hills overlooking Florence. He observed that while fashion seasons were fleeting, the tactile joy of full-grain vegetable-tanned leather was eternal. Henri committed his life to combining time-honored saddle-stitching with refined Parisian silhouettes.

Four decades later, Maison Vélure remains family-owned and artisan-led. We resist industrial mass manufacturing. Every single bag is cut by hand, edge-burnished with natural beeswax, and inspected through a twenty-point quality protocol before receiving our gold foil crest.

Conscious Luxury

Our Mission & Sustainable Vision

True luxury leaves no destructive footprint. We are devoted to ethical tanning and lifetime longevity.

100% Vegetable Tanning

Four Pillars of Perfection

The Anatomy of a Maison Vélure Bag

Take an intimate look at the four distinct stages required to bring our leather silhouettes to life.

01

Hide Selection

Only the top 5% of European full-grain hides are selected. We inspect for natural fiber density, grain uniformity, and organic warmth.

02

Hand-Skiving & Cutting

Master artisans hand-cut individual leather panels with surgical shears. Edges are skived down by microns to eliminate bulk along seams.

03

Double Saddle Stitch

Two needles pass through every pre-punched awl hole with beeswax-coated linen thread, creating an interlocking knot that cannot unravel.

04

Edge Lacquer & Polish

Seven successive layers of vegetable lacquer are applied by hand, hand-sanded between coats, and buffed to a smooth glass-like finish.
